If you, a child or a pet went in the water

People: rinse off with tap water right away. If you feel sick, contact a healthcare provider or Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.

Pets: rinse them with tap water right away, before they lick their fur. If they seem sick, call a veterinarian immediately. Animal poison lines (they charge a fee): ASPCA 1-888-426-4435 · Pet Poison Helpline 1-855-764-7661.

From the CDC's Harmful Algal Blooms and Your Health. Not medical advice.
